\# Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis



\## Cognifyz Technologies - Data Science Internship



\### Project Overview



This project performs exploratory data analysis on the Zomato

restaurant dataset to identify patterns related to restaurant

locations, cuisines, pricing, ratings, customer engagement,

online delivery, and table booking.



\## Objectives



\- Analyze restaurant distribution across cities.

\- Identify popular cuisines.

\- Analyze restaurant ratings.

\- Study price range and average cost.

\- Analyze online delivery availability.

\- Analyze table booking availability.

\- Study the relationship between ratings and votes.

\- Perform correlation analysis.

\- Generate business insights and recommendations.



\## Dataset



The dataset contains:



\- 9,551 restaurant records

\- 21 attributes



Important features include:



\- Restaurant Name

\- City

\- Cuisines

\- Average Cost for two

\- Has Table booking

\- Has Online delivery

\- Price range

\- Aggregate rating

\- Votes

The project analyzes:



\- Top restaurant cities

\- Rating distribution

\- Popular cuisines

\- Price range distribution

\- Average cost

\- Online delivery

\- Table booking

\- Rating vs price range

\- Rating vs votes

\- Top-rated restaurants

\- City-wise restaurant performance

\- Correlation between numerical variables



\## Business Insights



The analysis provides insights into restaurant distribution,

customer preferences, pricing patterns, service availability,

restaurant ratings, and customer engagement.



\## Recommendations



\- Optimize pricing based on customer expectations.

\- Improve digital ordering and delivery services where appropriate.

\- Monitor customer ratings and feedback.

\- Use location-based strategies.

\- Analyze cuisine demand before expansion.

\- Focus on customer engagement as well as ratings.



\## Limitations



\- The dataset represents information from a particular period.

\- Correlation does not imply causation.

\- Customer ratings can be subjective.

\- Detailed customer demographic and operational information is not

&#x20; available.

\- Restaurant representation varies across cities.

\- Cost comparisons across countries may be affected by different

&#x20; currencies.
